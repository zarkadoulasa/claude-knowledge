# Build a Brand-Aware Marketing Project

> **Provenance.** Timestamps link to [[Nate Herk - Claude as a One-Person Marketing Team]]. The approval gate (step 8), review checks and starter files are vault additions; supporting facts are under *Beyond the source*.

## Goal

A folder Claude opens as your marketing team: a brand brief, a short CLAUDE.md routing to context files, your real assets and a connected image and video platform. Nate's reason to work through Claude rather than the platform's own interface: the project carries business context, skills and brand guidelines ([02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=177s)).

## Use when

- You market a product and want on-brand ads, posts, a site or video without editing skills ([00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=0s)).
- Generic output is the problem. Nate says the three Ps keep assets from looking like AI slop ([02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=120s)).

## Prerequisites

- The Claude Code desktop app, signed in with a Claude subscription ([07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=463s)). "Code" doesn't mean coding ([05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=351s)).
- Your three Ps, product facts and angles.
- Logos, product shots and a brand-guidelines PDF. If you lack them, he suggests generating them first in Higgsfield, picking GPT Image 2 for a logo ([04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=265s)).
- A media platform on a paid plan; Nate uses [[Higgsfield]] ([16:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=987s)).

## Steps

1. **Write the three Ps.** Pain is the problem you solve, person is who specifically feels it, and promise is how you solve it for them ([01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=99s)). His fictional Perkform brief: a busy, fitness-minded professional; too many separate morning products; coffee and protein in one can ([03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=228s)).
2. **Open an empty folder.** Create it, start a new local chat, click Open folder, then Trust workspace so Claude can read, add, edit and move files there ([07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=433s), [08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=484s)).
3. **Brain-dump; let Claude write the context.** Say who you are, what the business does and that Claude is your marketing team, and ask for CLAUDE.md plus whatever context files it thinks useful ([08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=508s), [09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=545s)). Paste the three Ps, business details and angles ([09:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=571s)). Three dots > Files shows files appearing ([09:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=592s)).
4. **Optional: get interviewed.** Drag the Grill Me file into the chat to install it ([10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=653s)), then run it on your product and marketing ([11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=665s)).
5. **Review what Claude wrote.** Nate got context/, assets/ and output/ folders ([11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=694s)), an open-questions file ([13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=830s)), and context files for positioning, customer avatar, product facts, voice and copy, message bank, playbooks and landscape ([11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=715s)). CLAUDE.md just named the project and owner and said to read those files first ([12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=751s)). Check positioning says what the brand is *not* ([13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=793s)) and delete invented facts.
6. **Add assets; confirm Claude sees them.** Drag in logos, product shots and the guidelines PDF ([14:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=841s)). If the Files panel looks wrong, ask Claude what it sees and give the expected count ([15:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=904s)).
7. **Connect the media platform.** In Higgsfield, open MCP and CLI, pick Claude and copy the URL ([16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=992s)). In Claude: Customize > Connectors > Add custom connector, paste and name it ([16:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1001s)), then Connect, sign in and allow ([16:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1013s)).
8. **Gate spending before the first batch (vault addition).** Nate asks for the cost only after a run ([22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1357s)), with several paid jobs running in one chat ([22:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1322s)). Add the `ask` rule, spending rules and batch brief below so Claude estimates first.
9. **Brief one deliverable at a time.** His pattern: talk to it like a person, giving motivation, end goal, save location and a cost request ([24:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1442s)). Name the three Ps, as his site brief does ([18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1090s)), and add your ad expertise ([27:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1642s)). His costs: 18 image ads $3.43 ([27:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1656s)), a sizzle reel about $17.55 ([29:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1748s)), five carousels $9.56 ([30:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1857s)); UGC video cost most, amount not given ([31:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1870s)).
10. **Review every batch yourself.** Expect first passes ([20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1211s)): he got a wrong logo in the reel ([28:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1724s)) and salesy UGC ([33:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2007s)), which he'd fix by approving scripts before video ([33:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2012s)). Keep copy-free variants ([26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1618s)) so the next promotion swaps only copy or CTA ([27:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1625s)).
11. **Log runs in a creative tracker.** Claude built his Excel tracker in about eight minutes ([36:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2169s)): a generation log with generations billed, model, credits, dollars and status ([36:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2206s)), plus angle-performance, copy-bank and cost-summary tabs ([37:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2262s)). Update it after every run ([37:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2235s)). He also suggests using it as an intake queue: drop ideas in, then tell Claude to check the sheet and process anything new ([37:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2243s)).
12. **Promote liked formats into skills.** When a batch works, make it a skill so later ones match ([25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1507s)); turn dislikes into rules ([30:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1806s)). He builds none on camera. See [[Build a Skill from a Successful Run]].
13. **Review performance weekly.** His unbuilt vision: Claude reviews performance data weekly and makes more of what worked ([30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1848s)). Schedule the review prompt below ([[Schedule Recurring Claude Tasks]]).

## Starter files & prompts

> [!note] Vault starter content
> Original wording for this vault, not files shown in the video. Replace `<placeholders>`.

### Folder layout

```text
brand-marketing/
├── CLAUDE.md
├── .claude/settings.json        # ask rule for MCP calls
├── .claude/skills/              # formats you approved (step 12)
├── context/
│   ├── brand-positioning.md     # three Ps + what we are not
│   ├── customer-avatar.md
│   ├── product-facts.md         # the only source for claims
│   ├── voice-and-copy.md
│   └── open-questions.md
├── assets/                      # logos/, product-shots/, brand-guidelines.pdf
└── output/
    ├── <channel>/<campaign-YYYY-MM>/   # assets, copy, run-notes.md
    └── analytics/creative-tracker.xlsx
```

### `context/brand-positioning.md`

```markdown
# Brand positioning: <Brand>

## Person
Who exactly: <role, situation, daily habit>. What they use today instead: <...>

## Pain
In their words: <...>. When it hurts most: <moment>

## Promise
How <product> removes that pain for that person: <one sentence>
Proof we can show: <fact from product-facts.md>

## What we are not
- Not <wrong category or tone>
- Never claim <unsupported claim>

## Angles to test
| Angle | Pain it targets | Status: untested / winning / retired |
|---|---|---|
```

### `CLAUDE.md`

```markdown
# <Brand> marketing project
Owner: <name>. You are the marketing team for <brand>, <one-line product>.

## Read before writing anything
- Audience and positioning: `context/brand-positioning.md`, `context/customer-avatar.md`
- Facts and claims: `context/product-facts.md` only. If one is missing, leave a visible
  TODO and add it to `context/open-questions.md`.
- Voice: `context/voice-and-copy.md`
- Visual rules: `assets/brand-guidelines.pdf`. Use logo files as-is; never generate a logo.

## Spending
- Before any paid generation, list deliverables, models and estimated credits and
  dollars, then wait for "go". One paid batch at a time.
- Afterwards, report the actual cost and add a tracker row.

## Outputs
`output/<channel>/<campaign-YYYY-MM>/` with `run-notes.md`: brief, models, cost, fixes.

## Learning
When I correct you, propose where the lesson goes (skill, context file or this file)
and wait for my yes.
```

### `.claude/settings.json`

```json
{
  "permissions": {
    "ask": ["mcp__*"]
  }
}
```

This prompts before every MCP call. It's broad on purpose: in the desktop app a narrower `mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield` rule can silently miss (*Beyond the source*). Avoid bypass mode, which skips prompts.

### Batch brief

```text
Goal: <what this is for and where it will run>.
Audience: the person in context/brand-positioning.md. Open on their pain; show the promise.
Deliverables: <count> <format and orientation>, including <n> without copy.
What good looks like: <your expertise: hook, CTA, length, what to avoid>.
First: show the plan with estimated credits and dollars, then stop and wait.
After: save to output/<channel>/<campaign-YYYY-MM>/, check each asset against the guidelines
and product facts (logo, colours, claims), list failures, report cost, add a tracker row.
```

### Promote-to-skill and weekly-review prompts

```text
I approved output/<path>. From its brief, run notes and the assets I kept and rejected,
draft .claude/skills/<format>/SKILL.md: read positioning and guidelines first, estimate cost
before generating, log to the tracker, add "never" rules from my rejections. Show me first.
```

```text
Read the creative tracker. Fetch last week's results per run from <connector or export>
without copying raw metrics into context files. Rank angles and copy by <metric>, then
propose next week's briefs: three building on winners, one new test. Generate nothing.
```

## Done when

- [ ] Positioning has a person, pain, promise and a checked "what we are not" list.
- [ ] CLAUDE.md routes to every context file and states the spending rules.
- [ ] Claude lists every asset, matching your count.
- [ ] A generation call raises a permission prompt (no prompt means the rule missed).
- [ ] One batch ran end to end: estimate, your go, campaign folder, run notes, tracker row with real cost.
- [ ] An approved format is a skill that a fresh session uses.
- [ ] The weekly review is scheduled.

## Pitfalls

- **Reporting cost instead of approving it** (step 8). The credits are gone before the cost line arrives.
- **Trusting self-QA.** Claude rejected a UGC clip before reporting done ([32:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1941s)), yet the reel shipped a wrong logo (step 10). See [[Verification Before Done]].
- **Placeholder facts,** like the site's unfilled nutrition panel ([20:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1217s)).
- **A CLAUDE.md that grows daily.** Nate expects to change it daily or weekly ([12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=763s)). Route most lessons elsewhere; see [[Keep CLAUDE.md Lean]].
- **Unread third-party skills.** He has viewers drag in a downloaded skill ([10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=653s)) and hands Claude a third-party repo URL ([17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1047s)), with no step to read either. See [[Build vs Install Third-Party Skills]].
- **Fake-testimonial UGC.** His brief asks for organic-feeling videos of people reviewing the product ([24:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=1467s)); see the FTC rule under *Beyond the source*.
- **Live metrics in project files.** He'd write views and conversions into the sheet ([35:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCACmFTiCto&t=2120s)). Keep run IDs and fetch metrics at review time; see [[Context vs Connections]].

## Beyond the source

*Not from the video. Checked 2026-09-15.*

- **Higgsfield costs.** Agent connections need a paid subscription, and every agent generation deducts credits on any plan. There's no built-in spending cap; the help centre suggests stating a credit limit in the chat, which is only a prompt. Its blog instead says new accounts get free credits, and recommends "Always Allow" for the tools. [Help centre](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/integrations/how-do-i-connect-higgsfield-to-ai-agent), [blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Generate-AI-Videos-From-Claude-with-Higgsfield-MCP)
- **An enforced gate.** Claude Code enforces permission rules; CLAUDE.md only steers the model. Ask rules accept globs such as `mcp__*`, and `bypassPermissions` mode skips prompts. Connector tools are documented as `mcp__claude_ai_<server>__<tool>`, but an open issue (opened 2026-09-08) reports that Desktop local sessions name them by connection UUID, so name-based rules silently miss. An organization's per-tool `ask` setting doesn't reach the desktop app either. [Permissions docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions), [MCP docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp), [issue #92787](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/92787)
- **Connectors in Claude Code** load only under a claude.ai subscription login. The terminal route is `claude mcp add --transport http <name> <url>`; verify you trust each server. [Claude Code docs: MCP](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp)
- **Weekly review.** Local Desktop scheduled tasks offer a Weekly preset but run only while the app is open and the computer awake. [Claude Code docs: desktop scheduled tasks](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/desktop-scheduled-tasks)
- **AI testimonials.** The FTC's August 2024 final rule covers testimonials misrepresented as coming from someone who doesn't exist, including AI-generated ones, or who has no real experience of the product. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)
