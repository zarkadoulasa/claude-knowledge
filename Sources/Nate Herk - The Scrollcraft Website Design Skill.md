# Nate Herk - The Scrollcraft Website Design Skill

> **Creator:** [[Nate Herk]] · **Published:** 2026-08-22 · **Length:** 16:44 · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE)

## TL;DR

[[Nate Herk]] demos [[Scrollcraft]], a free [[Claude Code]] skill he wrote. It turns ordinary landing pages into scroll-driven sites, where the visitor's scroll controls what happens on the page. The skill first interviews you about the scroll journey, the one belief, your real assets, a signature move, and where the page should feel calm or intense. It generates missing images and video through Kie.ai, builds the page, then screenshots scroll keyframes to check itself. He redesigns his AI Automation Society site live. The first draft took about 30 minutes. One section-by-section feedback message then produced a v2 that kept the brand and copy. He judges quality by eye, and the self-check didn't flag a wrong caption, unexplained screenshots or a link that needed changing.

## Key takeaways

- **Tie the scroll to something on the page.** Scrolling scrubs images, fades numbers in or fades images out, and the visitor can scroll back and forth [07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=466s). Not every section needs heavy motion [01:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=113s). See [[Build a Scroll-Driven Landing Page]].
- **Variety comes from the brief, not a template.** The interview captures feel, journey and goals. Where you have no vision, the skill's built-in taste fills the gap [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=442s). See [[Escaping the Default AI Design Look]].
- **Start with a short prompt.** He gives a few adjectives and one anti-goal, then lets the interview ask the rest [04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=241s). See [[Plan Before Executing]].
- **Hook first.** He compares a landing page to short-form content [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=86s).
- **Describe feelings, not just features.** He says models now take emotional instruction better. He has the skill imagine being the visitor scrolling [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=496s). This is his impression, not something he tests.

## Caveats & disagreements

**About the video**

- **Self-promotion.** It's his own skill, and the only download route he gives is his free Skool community, which feeds a paid tier. The description's affiliate and promo links are omitted here.
- **Judged by eye.** There are no metrics and no side-by-side comparison, and the build itself is cut from the video.
- **Visual self-checks are not content checks.** The keyframe pass flagged none of the first-draft problems listed above. He caught them himself, so a human content review is still needed.
- **Not Claude Design.** Despite the title, this is a Claude Code skill, not [[Claude Design]].
- **Unvetted install.** He gives no advice to read the skill before installing it [02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=177s), yet it runs scripts and can call a paid API (see Beyond the source).
- **Low-poly default.** The current SKILL.md bans low-poly as a default style (see Beyond the source), yet he got low-poly scenes by asking [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=400s): the first draft's certification section has a generated low-poly video of figures teaching [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=678s). Either an explicit request overrides the default or the rules changed after the video *(uncertain)*.
- **Names withheld.** Community members' names shown on screen are omitted.

**Versus existing vault notes**

- **[[Build vs Install Third-Party Skills]].** Ras Mic won't install others' skills ([12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=759s)). Nate hands his out for direct install. Scrollcraft has scripts, network calls and an API key, so that note's read-first checks apply.
- **[[Escaping the Default AI Design Look]].** That note relies on references and a generate, refine and review chain of skills. Here a single skill uses an interview plus built-in taste [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=442s). There are no external inspiration sites, though his earlier Scrollcraft builds were in the project and he pointed to a subtle one as the vibe [04:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=266s). It backs [[AI LABS - Claude Design Skills for Beautiful Sites]], who say landing pages gain most from scroll animation ([02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ysr7oNDajJI&t=179s)).
- **[[Nate Herk - 32 Tricks to Level Up Claude Code]].** There he clones inspiration sites ([10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqoFP9QapXI&t=605s)) and has Claude scrap okay work ([08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqoFP9QapXI&t=484s)). Here he keeps what worked and sends targeted fixes [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUI6Ug4cHnE&t=739s).
- **[[Build a Distinctive Site with Design Skills]].** That technique runs one generator skill and a reviewer with a numeric pass bar: every dimension at least 8/10, three rounds at most. Nate reviews by eye in one round. Scrollcraft bundles the generator, asset pipeline and verifier, so it doesn't fit that technique's one-generator setup.
- **[[Verification Before Done]].** A refinement: screenshots show how a page looks, not whether its captions and links are right.
- **[[Plan Before Executing]].** The vault's version is a generic AskUserQuestion interview. Here the questions are specific to web design and built into the skill.

## Build from this

1. **Redesign one page with Scrollcraft.** Read its scripts first, start from owned assets to avoid spend, and record the interview answers. See [[Build a Scroll-Driven Landing Page]]. *Vault suggestions:* keep permission prompts on for its scripts and API calls ([[Permissions and Approval Gates]]), and use a fresh session per build ([[Context Window Management]]).
2. **Reusable site-intake brief.** Cover journey order, a one-sentence belief, owned vs generated assets with a style line, a signature move, an energy curve and a target feeling. See [[Build a Distinctive Site with Design Skills]] and [[Grill Me Interview Skill]].
3. **Motion-aware verification.** Screenshot several scroll positions and widths into a contact sheet, then compare how it feels against the brief. See [[Build Verification into Every Task]].
4. **"Claims have receipts" content audit.** Check every stat, caption and link against a source of truth before shipping. See [[Verification Before Done]].
5. **Section-by-section feedback template.** For each section, note keep, problem, fix and link source, then tick each item against v2. See [[Benchmark-Driven Design Critique]].
6. **Brand-matched generated assets.** Reuse one style line in every prompt, and inspect stills before animating them. See [[Generate On-Brand Images from Claude Code]].

## Beyond the source

*Not from the video. Checked 2026-09-15.*

- **Repo.** nateherkai/scroll-craft, MIT licence, v0.3.0. <https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft>
  - **Install in Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add nateherkai/scroll-craft`, then `/plugin install nateherk-design`.
  - **Requirements:** Node 18+, a full ffmpeg build, and playwright-core with Chrome.
  - **API key:** `KIE_AI_API_KEY` is optional. Owned photos and footage need no key and cost nothing.
  - **Testing:** the README says the ten example sites were built with [[OpenAI Codex]], and that the skill has only been run on Windows.
- **SKILL.md.** <https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft/blob/main/plugins/nateherk-design/skills/scroll-craft/SKILL.md>
  - **Intake:** eight topics. It adds vibe references, an emotional arc with one peak, and a choice between one continuous world and distinct scenes.
  - **Style:** worlds are photographic by default. Clay and low-poly are banned as defaults.
  - **Variety:** eight page grammars. A `FINGERPRINTS.md` registry requires each build to differ from past ones on at least 4 of 6 dimensions.
  - **Verification:** `shoot.mjs` captures desktop, 390×844 and reduced-motion runs into `sheet.png`, flagging dead scroll and poor contrast. The agent then compares one feeling word per act against `BRIEF.md`.
  - **Banned tells:** scroll cues, `01 / 06` counters, em dashes, gradient text, invented statistics.
- **Plugins.** The docs give the form `/plugin install <plugin>@<marketplace>`, and a bare name also works in a session. They warn that plugins can run arbitrary code with your privileges. <https://code.claude.com/docs/en/discover-plugins>
- **Kie.ai.**
  - **What it is:** an API aggregator for AI models. You send your key as a Bearer token. <https://docs.kie.ai/>
  - **Pricing and models (third-party review):** prepaid credits, with models including Veo, Kling and Nano Banana. <https://www.bitdoze.com/kie-ai-review/>
