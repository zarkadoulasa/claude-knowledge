## Ideas associated with him

- **Storage is where notes go to die** unless something brings them back ([00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=55s)). → [[Design for Retrieval]]
- **Three pillars:** a wiki of saved web content, a CRM of people he meets, and a daily journal ([01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=69s)). The knowledge base is the hub; swap the other layers for your own inputs ([02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=150s)). → [[Add a Journal and Personal CRM to a Second Brain]]
- **Grounded journaling:** replies draw on his saved knowledge, not generic chatbot advice ([04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=290s)).
- **Keep the scaffold minimal:** Codex built 51 files from Karpathy's gist until he told it to build only what the gist calls for ([11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=691s)). → [[Bootstrap an LLM Wiki from the Karpathy Gist]]
- **Trigger-word modes:** how a message starts decides whether the chat queries the wiki, writes a journal entry or updates the CRM ([23:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=1422s)).
- **Hourly unattended ingest** through a Codex automation ([29:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yke4fLQUsh4&t=1774s)). → [[Ingest Sources into an LLM Wiki]]
