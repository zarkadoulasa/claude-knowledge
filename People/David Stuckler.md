## Who

- **Channel:** "Prof. David Stuckler" on YouTube ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UChP5v-RPeEQP27rzIHnHu7w)).
- **Background he states:** a professor who has published 400+ peer-reviewed papers and reviews/publishes for a living ([00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=6s)).
- **What he teaches:** helping researchers publish in high-impact journals, through his consulting/mentorship business (Stuckler Consulting / FastTrack) linked in his video description. He shares free skills (e.g. a topic validator).
- **His angle on AI:** practical, ethics-forward research workflows — he stresses staying on the "right side of angels" and vetting tools rather than chasing sponsored products ([08:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=533s)), and says he takes no kickbacks ([02:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=139s)).

## Ideas associated with them

- **Connectors give Claude "vision."** The mental model for connectors as sight into tools and databases you already use ([01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=75s)). → [[Connecting Claude to External Tools]]
- **Ground research in real sources.** Research connectors solve the hallucinated-reference problem because you can open and verify the actual paper ([04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=296s)). → [[Grounding Research in Real Sources]]
- **Forensic vs browsing search.** Connectors (Consensus/PubMed) for targeted retrieval; Google Scholar for browsing by relevance and citations ([05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=312s)).
- **Skills capture SOPs.** Turn a repeated academic procedure into a customized prompt that runs every time, and have Claude build the skill for you ([06:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=389s)). → [[Agent Skills]]
- **Leverage over burnout.** Skills exist so early-stage researchers aren't stuck at one project, one paper, one burnt-out researcher ([06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWtx-d3dALQ&t=363s)).
