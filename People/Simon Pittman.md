## Ideas associated with him

- **Global instructions first.** He calls them the most important setup step, and the easiest to miss ([08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=492s)). They are rules read at the start of every conversation, in effect a CLAUDE.md file ([08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=534s)). → [[Set Up Claude Cowork]], [[CLAUDE.md as a Router]]
- **One dedicated workspace folder** for Cowork ([03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=226s)). → [[Permissions and Approval Gates]]
- **An About Me folder** ([12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=755s)). → [[Write Anti-AI Writing Rules]], [[Agent Memory Patterns]]
  - Writing rules that Claude builds from Wikipedia's page on AI writing style ([14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=870s)).
  - A memory.md that Claude appends to ([14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=895s)).
  - The global instructions must point to these files ([15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=916s)).
- **Make corrections stick.** After giving feedback, he has Claude update its notes ([26:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=1598s)). For email he relies on drafts only, plus a rule never to send without his check ([26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=1609s)).
- **A context map for Notion.** He saves a map of his Notion workspace ([29:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=1760s)); with it, Claude found the right database at once ([30:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=1833s)). → [[Build a Context Map for a Connected Tool]], [[Connecting Claude to External Tools]]
- **Skills versus plugins:** skills are tools in the toolbox; a plugin is a specialist who brings their own tools ([40:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=2437s)). → [[Agent Skills]]
- **Scheduled tasks** are his favourite feature ([41:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=2487s)). He says the computer must stay on ([41:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=2507s)); see *Beyond the source*. → [[Schedule Recurring Claude Tasks]]

## Where sources disagree

- **Always-loaded personal files.**
  - Simon's global instructions, read at the start of every conversation, point to all three About Me files ([15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl90LATQlHI&t=916s)).
  - [[Ras Mic - How AI Agents and Claude Skills Work]] says most people don't need always-loaded context files ([02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=124s)). His exception is company-specific or proprietary information, or a method of your own, that every conversation needs ([03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=188s)).
  - Vault reading: a stable profile fits that exception better than a memory log that keeps growing.
