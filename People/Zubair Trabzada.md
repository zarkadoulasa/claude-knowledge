# Zubair Trabzada

## Who

Zubair Trabzada is an AI-tutorial creator on the "AI Workshop" YouTube channel. In the vault's one source he is the sole narrator, presenting a hype-debunk explainer on Jev [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=0s). He works primarily with GPT-6 Astra and Claude Code as builder tools and demonstrates a personal AI second brain he calls "Jarvis" [08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=481s).

His content leans promotional — in the video he points viewers to a free PDF guide linked in the description [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=39s) — so his capability and pricing claims are worth treating as an enthusiast's framing rather than independent testing.

## In this vault

- [[Zubair Trabzada - What Jev Actually Is]] — creator. A myth-busting explainer arguing Jev is overhyped, that you can't build *with* it, and that its real role is a fast decision layer beside an LLM.

## Ideas associated with them

- **"Jev decides but does not write."** His central definition: Jev is a decision layer that returns odds, a yes/no, a pick-one, or a rating for a bounded set of choices, and never generates text [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=76s) [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=103s). See [[Jev]].
- **LLM-builds-the-app, Jev-as-the-middle-decision-layer.** His recommended architecture: use Claude Code or GPT-6 Astra to build and reason, and have Jev sit in the middle for the fast choice-type decisions — including replacing a slow Opus 5 retrieval call in his Jarvis assistant with a quick Jev routing decision [06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=368s) [08:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMV6JKkQVfE&t=511s). See [[System 1 and System 2 AI Models]].
