## Ideas associated with him

- **The new-employee comparison.** Teaching an agent a workflow is like onboarding a new hire ([13:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=817s)). He sums up Ras Mic's method in four steps: map the workflow, define right and wrong, iterate, then codify ([13:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=828s)). → [[Build a Skill from a Successful Run]]
- **Expect early hiccups.** He restates the point that a new skill won't work straight away and needs several rounds of fixes ([23:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=1403s)). → [[Skill Improvement Loop]]
- **The Office "rundown" example.** In a scene from the TV show, a new boss asks for a rundown and the employee doesn't know what that means. Greg uses it to show that an agent lacks the shared business context a colleague has ([24:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=1482s)). Ras Mic links it back to his point that context matters more than the model ([25:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_oN3vlzpMw&t=1526s)). → [[Agent Skills]]
