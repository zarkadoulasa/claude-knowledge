# Jack Roberts

## Who

- **Startup:** founded Glaido (captioned "Glider"), the subject of most demos ([04:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=299s)). Its design system spans presentations, website and app ([07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=455s)).
- **His Claude Code OS:** it has chat, a usage and spend dashboard, and a daily local review of his chats that suggests new skills ([14:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=889s)). His design OS is one module inside it ([13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=815s)).

## Ideas associated with him

- **Models design badly because they haven't seen great design** ([01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=64s)). Slop shows in five places: typography, imagery, hierarchy, colour and spacing ([01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=99s)). → [[Escaping the Default AI Design Look]]
- **Level 1, real design systems:** start from a gallery of 2,000+ product systems ([01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=118s)), then measure your own site against one ([04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=294s)). → [[Design Systems for Claude]], [[Benchmark-Driven Design Critique]]
- **Level 2, the design loop:** his name for the Gauntlet Loop ([08:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=520s)). Critic subagents check the work against a benchmark in rounds ([09:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=599s)). → [[Multi-Agent Review and Scoring Loops]], [[Loop Engineering]]
- **Zapier as one authentication layer** shared by every harness ([12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=733s)). → [[Connecting Claude to External Tools]]
- **Level 3, an environment to work in** ("one engine, one box"), his favourite level ([13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=788s)). → [[Agentic OS]]
- **Style recipes:** a saved description plus reference images, reused for consistent thumbnails ([16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAumQObJEwM&t=969s)). → [[Generating Images and Video with Claude]], [[Higgsfield]]
