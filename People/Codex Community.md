# Codex Community

## Who

Codex Community is a YouTube channel host who publishes hands-on reviews of AI coding and design tools. The channel name is misleading: despite "Codex" (which evokes OpenAI Codex), the content captured in this vault is a review of **Claude Opus 5.5** on web design, 3D, and animation. Per the video description, the creator also runs the **EnhanceUI** web-design course (external; not detailed on camera). He is not named in the transcript, so no further biographical detail is recorded here.

## In this vault

- [[Codex Community - Testing Opus 5.5 on Design and 3D]] — creator. Single-reviewer test of Claude Opus 5.5 on four of his own design/3D prompts plus third-party community demos.

## Ideas associated with them

- **Reusable benchmark prompts.** He keeps a fixed set of personal prompts (award-winning site, 3D space-agency site) and re-runs them on each new model to compare output quality qualitatively over time.
- **"Slow but stunning" delegate-and-wait.** Treats a slow, always-thinking model like a person you hand a task to and wait on — ~1 hour per complex prompt as an accepted cost for near-professional output.
- **Redesign-a-live-site as an eval task.** Pointing the model at a real, existing site (typeui.sh) and asking for a restyled version tests design judgment against a concrete baseline.
